Why not just a form?

A form collects answers and then waits. Someone has to open the spreadsheet, notice what matters, and tell the right person. That step is where it always breaks down.

A form waits to be read. This tells the Pastor who needs attention before he thinks to ask — the visitor nobody called, the leader who has missed three Sundays.

Nothing is retyped. Paper gets lost between the usher and the office, and whatever survives has to be entered by hand. Here it arrives already in the record.

Almost nobody signs in. Members and outreach teams just tap a link. Only the Pastor and the secretary have an account — so there are no passwords for 150 people to forget.

Each outreach builds on the last. Right now every one starts from nothing. After a year you can see who was reached, who was visited, and who is now sitting in church.
